Luciano Stork

Close to discovering something I still cannot name.

Most people think of wireless signals as a way to communicate. I can't help wondering what else they can tell us.

I'm Luciano, a Brazilian telecommunications engineer and PhD student in Computer Science at Rowan University. Somewhere between electromagnetic waves, signal processing, and sensing, I find myself chasing questions that often lead to more questions than answers.

This is where I share some of them, along with the experiments, ideas, and occasional surprises along the way.
